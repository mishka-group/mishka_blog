# Changelog for MishkaBlog 0.0.3

- The Analytics page reads its tiles (Total Views, Views in Period, Unique Readers, Views Today, Engagement), its chart and its top posts from `PostViewDaily`, and no longer opens on a roadmap notice. Unique Readers says it is approximate when a site runs on several servers. The Referrers and Audience panels are gone, and so is `MishkaBlog.PostView` with its `mishka_blog_post_views` table, which nothing wrote to and which kept a reader's IP: a host drops the table with its next `mix ash.codegen`. The dev seeds write daily view rows
- The Blog dashboard's Today's Views, Engagement, the Analytics card's Total Views and Growth Rate and This Month's Total Views and Engagement Rate are read from `PostViewDaily`, each one `SUM` (`Dashboard.Scope.sum/2`) off the render, and lose their "Not tracked yet" label; the Top posts widget ranks posts by their views ("Most viewed") and the Recent posts widget shows them, without a roadmap hint
- A post's views are counted with no database write per view: `Plugins.PostViewCount` (on `RuntimeLive.HandleParams.AfterCompile`) counts a view when a post's page is shown to a connected reader, once per post per LiveView, so a crawler that runs no JavaScript never counts, and `Plugins.PostViewReader` (on `RuntimeLive.OnMount.AfterCompile`) keeps the reader's user agent, when the host's `/live` socket lists `:user_agent` in its `connect_info`, and session. A user agent naming a bot, crawler, script or headless browser does not count, and a plugin on `blog:post:count_view` (`%{count?:, site_id:, post_id:, user_agent:, current_user:}`) may change that. `MishkaBlog.Views` counts in a node's ETS, a reader counted once a day by a salted hash of their account or session (no IP is read), drops hits once it holds `max_keys` (100,000), and every `flush_every` (60 seconds) writes what it counted with one bulk upsert into the new `mishka_blog_post_views_daily` table (`MishkaBlog.PostViewDaily`, one row per site, post and day, `views = views + n`), putting the numbers back when the write fails and flushing when it stops. After a write it fires `blog:post:viewed` once with the batch, `%{views: [%{site_id:, post_id:, day:, views:, unique_readers:}]}`. `PostViewDaily`'s `:purge_expired` deletes the days older than the new global setting `blog.views.retention_days` (730), run every night by AshOban on the `:maintenance` queue (`ash_oban` is a dependency). A post's `view_count` is the sum of its days
- Can Publish Directly is enforced: a blogger whose profile on the post's site does not have it cannot publish or schedule a post, by its action or by saving it as published or scheduled, and is told "Your blogger profile can't publish directly. Save the post as a draft for an editor to publish." A master, a user holding the new `admin:dashboard:blog:publish` (`admin:api:blog:publish` on the API, or a wildcard covering it) and an editor with no blogger profile are not blocked, and neither is `PublishScheduledWorker`. A plugin on `blog:post:can_publish` is handed `%{allowed?: boolean, actor:, site_id:, blogger:, changeset:}` and may change `allowed?` (`Resources.Validations.PublishDirectly`)
- A scheduled post is published at its time: `MishkaBlog.Workers.PublishScheduledWorker` (queue `:maintenance`) publishes every due post of every site with one bulk `:master_publish` over the new `:due_scheduled` read, its `published_at` the scheduled time, and the publish notifies as any other does. The host runs it on its Oban crontab, `{"* * * * *", MishkaBlog.Workers.PublishScheduledWorker}`. Publishing a post, by its action or by saving it as published, also clears its scheduled time, so a post published before its time can still be saved after that time has passed. The unused `auto_publish_scheduled_posts()` SQL function is gone, the post form and the drafts widget no longer say publishing at the scheduled time is on the roadmap, and a schedule's agent receipt checks that the job is on the crontab (`:not_on_crontab` when it is not)
- A post's, a category's, a tag's, a blogger's or a team's old address keeps working after a save moves it (a new slug, language, date, number, parent category or first category): the save writes a 301 from the old address to the new one as a site redirect rule (`MishkaCmsCore.Runtime.Redirect`), listed in Admin, Redirects. Redirects to the old address are pointed at the new one, so there are no chains, a rule at the new address is archived, and an active rule already at the old address is never overwritten, while an inactive one there is pointed at the new address and switched on. A draft writes nothing, and a blog editor needs no redirect permission (`Resources.Changes.KeepOldAddress`)
- A post asked for under a category it is not filed in, or by a date or number that is not its own, or at the slug page while the site's post page names `:category`, is sent to its own address with a 301 (`Plugins.PostMoved` on `runtime:page:moved`). Under another of its categories it is still its page, whose canonical is its first category's address, each language edition at its own. Needs the `runtime:page:moved` event of mishka_cms_core
- A site chooses where its posts are read in Settings, Blog, Addresses, **Post address**: `/blog/<post>` (the default) or `/blog/<category>/<post>`. Saving the second gives the site an active post page at `/:lang/blog/:category/:slug` one priority above the slug page, through the Page resource's own update as the admin who saved it; the slug page stays for posts filed under nothing. Saving the first again makes that page inactive (`Plugins.PostAddress`). `Plugins.FeedSettings` is `Plugins.BlogSettings`, which registers both groups
- A post's breadcrumb on a page whose path names `:category` is Home, Blog, its first category, the post
- The posts, categories and tags a page is previewed with in the editor are in the languages the site publishes in, so no sample opens a page the site does not serve
- A post's Publish button shows only on a draft or a scheduled post, and its Unpublish button only on a published or a scheduled post, and unpublishing takes away its scheduled time too, so a scheduled post sent back to draft is never published by its old schedule
- Blog post, tag, collection, blogger and team slugs are made by `mishka_slug` instead of `ash_slug`: a slug keeps the scripts of the site's languages (a Persian title stays Persian on a site that publishes in Persian) instead of being transliterated, accents are removed from Latin text, `_` is kept, and `.`, `!` and `?` are no longer kept
- The tag grid sizes only the trigger of a tag's actions menu (`[aria-haspopup]`), not every button in the wrapper, so the item buttons of the menu gervaz draws as a popover keep their size
- An extension adds buttons to the header of the Blog dashboard on the `blog:dashboard:actions` event. A plugin is handed `%{actions: [], current_user: user}` and answers `%{actions: [...]}` with an `id`, `label`, `icon`, `priority` and a `path:` or a `js:` command
- A comment an admin creates keeps the author it names, a user of the same site, where it was always stored as the admin's own. A comment that names no author (or a `nil` one) is still the admin's own, and an author from another site is refused
- A comment can be a guest's: a guest's name, and the email they gave, in place of a user author. The name and email are plain text, their tags taken out. Only the admin's create writes them, a comment with neither an author nor a guest's name is refused, and so is a bad email. The post names a guest, and the Comments page shows a guest's email to moderators, the only readers who ever get it
- The post content scrubber keeps `figure` and `figcaption`, the `srcset` and `sizes` of an image, and the `allowfullscreen` and `allow` of an `iframe`, and drops `<style>` and `<object>`
- A post's, a category's, a tag's, a blogger's or a team's page whose address names one the site does not have answers 404 instead of a page saying it is not here, as WordPress does: the page's own read answers it missing (`MishkaCmsCore.Runtime.PageSource.missing/0`), so the next page that matches the address is tried first and the site's 404 comes last. A real category or tag with nothing published under it is still its own empty page, and a post locked to permissions the reader does not hold still answers with its lock
- A site keeps the addresses it had on WordPress: the post page's path may be `/:slug`, `/:year/:monthnum/:slug`, `/:year/:monthnum/:day/:slug`, `/:category/:slug`, `/archives/:post_id`, with or without a `/:lang` or an `/index.php` prefix, and the category, tag and blogger pages' paths whatever the site used. A date, a number or a category in the address must be the post's, or the address is missing. `:category` is one segment, the category's own slug; WordPress's whole chain (`/parent/child/slug`) is not an address
- Every link the blog builds to a post, a category, a tag, a blogger or a team, in its blocks, the head, the language switcher and the sitemap, is the address of the site's page for that kind of record, filled from the row (`MishkaBlog.Address`): the provisioned pages no longer write `post_base`, `category_base`, `tag_base`, `author_base`, `team_base` or `chips_base`, and a block given none links at the site's page. A base an author writes still wins. `blog-post-list` and `blog-featured-posts` open a post beside themselves only with `open_beside`, `blog-post-list` draws its category chips with `show_chips`, and `blog-post-detail` takes its way back in `back_href`. A site takes them by upgrading the blog components from the "Blog components" card (kit 3.12.0)
- A category under another is listed, counted and paged at its whole chain, `/category/design/accessibility`, on a category page whose path ends in `*slug`: `blog-post-list`, `blog-featured-posts` and `blog-post-search` read a `collection` given as a chain by its last segment, and `blog-category-list`, `blog-post-detail` and every other block link a category at its chain there. The leaf alone, `/category/accessibility`, still answers, with the chain as its canonical address. The breadcrumb's Blog is the site's page for the list of posts (`MishkaBlog.Address.page_url/3`) instead of the parent of the page's path. A site takes the blocks by upgrading the blog components from the "Blog components" card (kit 3.13.0)
- A post has a `number`, unique on its site, its `/archives/:post_id` address: a new post gets the next free one, and a create or an update may name its own, which is how an import keeps a WordPress post's id
- A post whose address would be a page the site already serves, `/about` on a site whose post page is `/:slug`, is refused with "This post's address, /about, is already a page of this site."
- `MishkaBlog.Provision.ensure_pages!/3` gives a site only the sample pages a caller names, each at a path of the caller's, with the components and the rest `ensure!/3` gives, so a site that moved some of its Blog pages gets the missing ones without a second copy of the others
- The lock is on the newest Hex releases, including `guarded_struct` 0.1.5, `lazy_html` 0.1.14 and `localize` 1.6.1
- The blog serves RSS 2.0, Atom 1.0 and JSON Feed 1.1 feeds of a site's newest posts at `/feed` (also `/feed.rss`), `/feed/atom` and `/feed.json`, and of a category, a tag or a blogger at `/feed/:kind/:key` with `/atom` or `/json` after it, a nested category also by its whole chain (`/feed/category/design/accessibility`), each under a language too (`/fa/feed`). They are `MishkaBlogWeb.Routes.routes(:api)` and `MishkaBlogWeb.FeedController`, built by `BlogPost`'s generic `:feed` action from one read of the posts a guest may open, at the address of the site's post page, with their authors, categories and rendered text. The HTML goes in CDATA and characters XML 1.0 forbids are taken out (`xml_builder` is a dependency). Each feed has a content hash `ETag` and answers `304`, carries the newest post's `last-modified`, is kept in `AssetsState` for five minutes and dropped on a write to a post, category, tag or blogger (`Resources.Notifiers.FeedRefresh`). An unknown term or a bad address is a `404` that says `Not Found`. Settings `blog.feeds.enabled`, `blog.feeds.items` (20) and `blog.feeds.full_content` are in a Feeds group of the Blog tab, and the head of a site's pages names its feeds, a category's, tag's or blogger's page also naming that term's (`Plugins.FeedLinks`). `blog-post-list` draws an RSS link beside its sort button to the feed of what it shows (`show_feed`, `feed_text`; none in a compact list) and `blog-post-detail` one in the line under the title, kit 3.14.0
- A category's and a tag's page has its own `<h1>`, the term's name in the page's language, with a category's description under it, where every one of them had the blog's "Notes, guides & long reads.": the provisioned page reads it from the row it already read for its address (`page_source`), so it costs no query, and the category's list is no longer titled with the name a second time. The blog's front page keeps its title, and a blogger's page keeps the blogger's name as its one `<h1>`
- Needs mishka_cms_core 0.0.5-alpha.13 (a page's event, info and state handlers keep their code signature with `CODE_SIGNING_KEY` set), mishka_gervaz 0.0.8 and guarded_struct 0.1.7. Every dependency is at its latest release, including ash 3.37.4 and spark 2.7.10
- Agent receipts for the blog's writes (`MishkaBlog.Plugins.AgentOperations`, on the core's `agent:operations` event): a post's create, update, publish, unpublish, schedule, archive, unarchive, permanent delete and version restore, and a category's and tag's create, update, archive, unarchive and permanent delete, each with its master twin. `MishkaCmsCore.Agent.Receipts.run/3` returns the receipt beside the result: the row read back from the table, the broadcast, the sitemap job, and for a publish the share-card job, the site's feeds dropped and the posts page that received it (`UiObserver` on the posts page; `ReceiptHook` on `BlogPost`, so the commit is heard after the transaction). A schedule's receipt is partial: nothing in the CMS publishes a scheduled post when its time comes, so that stage stays unverified (`:nothing_publishes_it`)

# Changelog for MishkaBlog 0.0.2

- The visual editor now comes from the `mishka_cms_builder` package instead of `mishka_cms_core`. The blog runs on `mishka_cms_core` 0.0.4, `mishka_gervaz` 0.0.3 and `mishka_installer` 0.1.15 from Hex
- A `blog-notice` dropped on a page from the palette says "Nothing here yet." instead of drawing an empty box
- A blogger, team or post block placed on a page whose address names none draws nothing for a reader. The line that tells the author what the block needs shows only in the editor and the preview
- The release runs on Erlang 29.1.2 and Elixir 1.20.5. The temporary OTP 29.0.5 pin is gone
- `igniter_js` is 0.5.2 and every dependency is at its latest release, including `phoenix_html` 4.4.0, which fixes a security advisory, and `jose` 1.11.13, which fixes 8 advisories
- Dependencies are audited with `hex.audit` and `deps.audit`, the workflows use a read-only `GITHUB_TOKEN`, and Dependabot runs weekly
