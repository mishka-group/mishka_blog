# Changelog for MishkaBlog 0.0.3

- Blog post, tag, collection, blogger and team slugs are made by `mishka_slug` instead of `ash_slug`: a slug keeps the scripts of the site's languages (a Persian title stays Persian on a site that publishes in Persian) instead of being transliterated, accents are removed from Latin text, `_` is kept, and `.`, `!` and `?` are no longer kept
- The tag grid sizes only the trigger of a tag's actions menu (`[aria-haspopup]`), not every button in the wrapper, so the item buttons of the menu gervaz draws as a popover keep their size
- An extension adds buttons to the header of the Blog dashboard on the `blog:dashboard:actions` event. A plugin is handed `%{actions: [], current_user: user}` and answers `%{actions: [...]}` with an `id`, `label`, `icon`, `priority` and a `path:` or a `js:` command
- A comment an admin creates keeps the author it names, a user of the same site, where it was always stored as the admin's own. A comment that names no author (or a `nil` one) is still the admin's own, and an author from another site is refused
- A comment can be a guest's: a guest's name, and the email they gave, in place of a user author. The name and email are plain text, their tags taken out. Only the admin's create writes them, a comment with neither an author nor a guest's name is refused, and so is a bad email. The post names a guest, and the Comments page shows a guest's email to moderators, the only readers who ever get it
- The post content scrubber keeps `figure` and `figcaption`, the `srcset` and `sizes` of an image, and the `allowfullscreen` and `allow` of an `iframe`, and drops `<style>` and `<object>`
- A post's, a category's, a tag's, a blogger's or a team's page whose address names one the site does not have answers 404 instead of a page saying it is not here, as WordPress does: the page's own read answers it missing (`MishkaCmsCore.Runtime.PageSource.missing/0`), so the next page that matches the address is tried first and the site's 404 comes last. A real category or tag with nothing published under it is still its own empty page, and a post locked to permissions the reader does not hold still answers with its lock
- A site keeps the addresses it had on WordPress: the post page's path may be `/:slug`, `/:year/:monthnum/:slug`, `/:year/:monthnum/:day/:slug`, `/:category/:slug`, `/archives/:post_id`, with or without a `/:lang` or an `/index.php` prefix, and the category, tag and blogger pages' paths whatever the site used. A date, a number or a category in the address must be the post's, or the address is missing. `:category` is one segment, the category's own slug; WordPress's whole chain (`/parent/child/slug`) is not an address
- Every link the blog builds to a post, a category, a tag, a blogger or a team, in its blocks, the head, the language switcher and the sitemap, is the address of the site's page for that kind of record, filled from the row (`MishkaBlog.Address`): the provisioned pages no longer write `post_base`, `category_base`, `tag_base`, `author_base`, `team_base` or `chips_base`, and a block given none links at the site's page. A base an author writes still wins. `blog-post-list` and `blog-featured-posts` open a post beside themselves only with `open_beside`, `blog-post-list` draws its category chips with `show_chips`, and `blog-post-detail` takes its way back in `back_href`. A site takes them by upgrading the blog components from the "Blog components" card (kit 3.12.0)
- A category under another is listed, counted and paged at its whole chain, `/category/design/accessibility`, on a category page whose path ends in `*slug`: `blog-post-list`, `blog-featured-posts` and `blog-post-search` read a `collection` given as a chain by its last segment, and `blog-category-list`, `blog-post-detail` and every other block link a category at its chain there. The leaf alone, `/category/accessibility`, still answers, with the chain as its canonical address. The breadcrumb's Blog is the site's page for the list of posts (`MishkaBlog.Address.page_url/3`) instead of the parent of the page's path. A site takes the blocks by upgrading the blog components from the "Blog components" card (kit 3.13.0)
- A post has a `number`, unique on its site, its `/archives/:post_id` address: a new post gets the next free one, and a create or an update may name its own, which is how an import keeps a WordPress post's id
- A post whose address would be a page the site already serves, `/about` on a site whose post page is `/:slug`, is refused with "This post's address, /about, is already a page of this site."
- The lock is on the newest Hex releases, including `guarded_struct` 0.1.5, `lazy_html` 0.1.14 and `localize` 1.6.1

# Changelog for MishkaBlog 0.0.2

- The visual editor now comes from the `mishka_cms_builder` package instead of `mishka_cms_core`. The blog runs on `mishka_cms_core` 0.0.4, `mishka_gervaz` 0.0.3 and `mishka_installer` 0.1.15 from Hex
- A `blog-notice` dropped on a page from the palette says "Nothing here yet." instead of drawing an empty box
- A blogger, team or post block placed on a page whose address names none draws nothing for a reader. The line that tells the author what the block needs shows only in the editor and the preview
- The release runs on Erlang 29.1.2 and Elixir 1.20.5. The temporary OTP 29.0.5 pin is gone
- `igniter_js` is 0.5.2 and every dependency is at its latest release, including `phoenix_html` 4.4.0, which fixes a security advisory, and `jose` 1.11.13, which fixes 8 advisories
- Dependencies are audited with `hex.audit` and `deps.audit`, the workflows use a read-only `GITHUB_TOKEN`, and Dependabot runs weekly
